## A. Google Search Console (30 minute, sabse pehle)

1. https://search.google.com/search-console → **Add property → Domain** → `storiesbyfoot.com` → DNS TXT record add karke verify.
2. **Sitemaps** → `sitemap.xml` → Submit.
3. **URL Inspection** me ye URLs ek-ek karke daalo → **Request indexing** (daily limit hoti hai, 8–10 roz):

```
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh/khardungla-changla-loop
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh/indias-last-turn
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh/migla-throne-ride
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh/ultimate-umlingla
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/destinations/ladakh/trans-himalayan-ride
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/ladakh-bike-trip-cost
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/manali-to-leh-vs-srinagar-to-leh-bike-trip
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/best-time-for-ladakh-bike-trip
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/ladakh-inner-line-permit-guide
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/ladakh-bike-trip-for-beginners
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/spiti-valley-bike-trip-guide
https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/sikkim-travel-guide-nathu-la-gangtok
```
4. **Bing**: https://www.bing.com/webmasters → "Import from Google Search Console".
   Phir deploy ke baad terminal me: `node scripts/indexnow.cjs` (Bing/Yandex ko 45 URLs ek saath bhejta hai).
5. Har hafte **Pages** report dekho: "Not indexed" me kya hai aur kyun.

## C. Reviews kaise maangein (10 → 50 reviews ka target)

Google Business Profile me **"Get more reviews"** link copy karo. Trip ke **last din** ya wapas aane ke **2 din baad** bhejo.

**WhatsApp message (Hinglish):**
> Hi [Name]! Aapke saath Ladakh ride karke bahut maza aaya 🏍️⛰️ Agar trip pasand aaya ho to 1 minute nikaal kar Google par review likh do, isse hamari team ko bahut help milti hai 🙏
> 👉 [REVIEW LINK]

**English:**
> Hi [Name], it was a pleasure riding with you! If you enjoyed the trip, a quick Google review would mean the world to our small team. 🙏 [REVIEW LINK]

Rules: sirf **asli customers** se maangein, review ke badle paisa/discount na dein (Google policy), aur har review ka reply dein.

## E. Link-building outreach (har hafte 5 emails)

Targets: travel bloggers, Royal Enfield / rider communities, Ladakh & Himachal travel blogs, college adventure clubs, local news (Mohali/Chandigarh).

**Template 1 — Guest post / resource link**
> Subject: Ladakh bike trip cost 2027 — resource for your readers
> Hi [Name],
> I read your post on [their post]. We recently published a price breakdown of Ladakh bike trips with what's included and what to ask operators: https://www.storiesbyfoot.com/blog/ladakh-bike-trip-cost
> If it helps your readers, would you consider linking to it? Happy to share data or photos for your next post too.
> Thanks, [Name] — Stories by Foot

**Template 2 — Collab / review**
> Subject: Invite to ride with Stories by Foot
> Hi [Name], we run guided Ladakh bike trips and would love to host you on a departure in exchange for an honest review/video. Interested? — [Name], +91 62051 29118

**Template 3 — Broken-link**
> Hi [Name], your page [URL] links to [dead link]. We have a current guide on the same topic that could replace it: [our URL].
